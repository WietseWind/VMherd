2. The VMherd app

The app does not collect personal data and does not send any data to The Integrators BV. It contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no crash reporting and no update checks, and it does not require an account.

Network connections

VMherd connects only to the Proxmox VE servers that you add yourself: over HTTPS to their API, and over a secure WebSocket for the console streams (usually port 8006). It opens no listening sockets and contacts no other servers. What you see on the consoles and what you type travels only between your Mac and your own servers.

What is stored on your Mac

  • Settings: your cluster bookmarks (name, API URL, API token ID), the pinned certificate fingerprints, the grid layout per cluster and your preferences. These are stored locally in the app's sandbox container on your Mac.
  • Token secrets: stored only in your macOS Keychain, as the app's own Keychain items, never in the settings file. Deleting a bookmark removes its secret from the Keychain.

This data stays on your Mac. We cannot see it and never receive it. To remove it all, delete your bookmarks in the app first (this removes their Keychain items), then delete the app. Moving the app to the Trash does not always remove its container: the settings may stay in ~/Library/Containers/app.vmherd until you delete that folder. Leftover Keychain items can be removed with the Keychain Access app.

Your Proxmox servers

Your Proxmox VE servers log their own activity, for example a console task for every console that VMherd opens. Those logs are on your servers and under your control; we have no access to them.

3. Buying through Apple

VMherd is sold and delivered by Apple through the Mac App Store. Apple processes your purchase and payment under its own privacy policy; we do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple gives developers aggregated sales reports and, only if you chose to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, usage statistics and crash reports. These do not identify you to us.

4. This website

vmherd.app is a static website. It sets no cookies, uses no local storage for tracking, runs no analytics and loads nothing from third parties: fonts, images and scripts are served from vmherd.app itself.

Like any web server, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, date and time, requested page, browser user agent and referring page) to operate and secure the service. We do not use these logs to identify visitors or build profiles, and they are deleted after a limited period. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Links to the Mac App Store and GitHub take you to those services, where their own privacy policies apply.

5. Support email

If you email support@vmherd.app, we use your email address and the contents of your message to answer you and to handle your request (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We keep this correspondence only as long as needed for that, and for any legal record-keeping obligations. We do not share it with others, except with the provider that hosts our email on our behalf.

Please do not send us API token secrets, passwords or other secrets. If you share screenshots or logs, remove hostnames and addresses you would rather keep private.

6. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or erased. You may also ask us to restrict processing, object to processing, or receive your data in a portable format. To use these rights, email support@vmherd.app. Since the app stores nothing with us, in practice this concerns support correspondence only.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Netherlands this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (Dutch Data Protection Authority), autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

7. Children

VMherd is a tool for server administration and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. If a change would affect how the app handles data, we will describe it in the app's release notes.
